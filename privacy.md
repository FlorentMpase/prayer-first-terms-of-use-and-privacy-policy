# Privacy Policy — Prayer First

**Effective date:** 8 August 2026
**Owner:** Florent Mpase (sole trader), United Kingdom
**Contact:** ronnympaseministries@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Prayer First ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Prayer First app. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

## 1. Who we are
Prayer First is a Christian focus app that helps you put God first by locking distracting apps until you pray, using Apple's Screen Time / Family Controls. It also offers guided prayer, Bible reading, live prayer rooms, and prayer content. The app is operated by Florent Mpase (sole trader), based in the United Kingdom.

## 2. Information we collect
Unlike some apps, Prayer First uses online accounts and features, so we do collect and process some data. Specifically:

**Account information**
- When you create an account, we store information needed to run it (such as your chosen name/display name and a user identifier) through our backend provider, Supabase.

**Prayer and app activity**
- Data related to your use of the app, such as prayer completion, streaks, prayer goals, lock settings, and content you generate or interact with, stored to make the app work for you.

**Live audio (prayer rooms and live prayer)**
- When you join or host a live prayer room or live prayer session, your audio is transmitted in real time through our audio provider, Agora, so other participants can hear you. We do not use this audio for any other purpose.

**Analytics (anonymous)**
- We collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (such as which screens are viewed and which features are used) through PostHog to understand how the app is used and improve it. This analytics data is not linked to your identity and does not include the content of your prayers, messages, or notes.

**Purchases and subscriptions**
- Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use Superwall to present subscription options and manage subscription status. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Apple handles all payment processing.

**Prayer content generation**
- Some prayer content in the app is generated using a third-party AI service (Anthropic). Where this is used, the request is processed to produce prayer text. We do not use this to build a profile of you.

**Video content**
- Prayer videos are hosted and delivered through Bunny Stream. When you stream a video, Bunny may process your IP address in order to deliver the video to you.

**Device and technical information**
- Standard technical information such as device type, operating system version, and crash/diagnostic data (via Apple) may be collected to keep the app stable and secure.

## 3. How we use your information
We use your information to:
- Provide and operate the app's features (locking, prayer, rooms, Bible, videos).
- Maintain your account, streaks, goals, and settings.
- Enable live audio prayer with other users.
- Process subscriptions and manage access to paid features.
- Understand usage and improve the app (via anonymous analytics).
- Keep the app safe, secure, and compliant, including acting on reports of objectionable content.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

## 4. Third-party services
We use trusted third-party providers to run the app. Each processes data only as needed to provide their service:
- **Apple** — App Store purchases, Screen Time / Family Controls, diagnostics.
- **Supabase** — accounts, database, and app data storage.
- **Agora** — real-time audio for live prayer rooms and live prayer.
- **Superwall** — subscription/paywall presentation and management.
- **PostHog** — anonymous, aggregated product analytics (EU-hosted).
- **Anthropic** — AI generation of prayer content.
- **Bunny Stream** — hosting and delivery of prayer videos.

These providers have their own privacy practices. We only share what is necessary for them to provide their service.

## 5. Live rooms, user content, and safety
Prayer First includes live prayer rooms where users interact. We have a zero-tolerance policy for objectionable, abusive, or harmful content and behaviour. Users can report and block others, and mute participants. We aim to review and act on reports within 24 hours, which may include removing content and restricting or removing users. See our Terms of Use for full details.

## 6. Data retention
We keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the app. Reports and safety-related records may be retained as needed to keep the community safe and meet our obligations.

## 7. Your rights and choices
You can:
- Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.
- Request deletion of your account and associated data.
To make a request, contact us at ronnympaseministries@gmail.com. Depending on your location (for example, under UK GDPR), you may have additional rights regarding your personal data.

## 8. Children's privacy
Prayer First is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. Parents/guardians are responsible for supervising minors' use of the app.

## 9. Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
